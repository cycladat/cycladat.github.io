cycladat is free to use. Connecting Strava, importing a FIT file, the
editor, the standard stickers, styling, templates and exporting all cost
nothing, for as many videos as you like. A few stickers are premium, and
that's the whole of it.

## What's premium

Right now: **power**, **heart rate** and **gradient**. They wear a rainbow
jersey in the drawer, and so do templates that include one. The set is
something we can adjust without an app update, so the jersey in the app is
the current truth rather than this page.

Premium stickers can be placed, moved and styled for free. The gate is at
export. If any premium sticker is on the video when you tap create, the app
offers to unlock it. Take the premium stickers off and the export is free
again.

## The options

- **This export only.** A one-off purchase that covers the export you're
  about to do. Good for the occasional big ride.
- **Monthly** and **annual** subscriptions. Unlimited premium exports while
  active. Annual is the cheaper rate.

Prices come from the App Store or Google Play in your currency and show on
the paywall. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them, and
cancelling is done in your App Store or Google Play account settings, not in
cycladat. Apple and Google both make you do it that way.

If every option on the paywall is greyed out and says **Unavailable**, the
store didn't return the products to the app. That's usually a network hiccup
or a store outage rather than anything in the app; try again in a minute.

## Restore purchases

If you reinstall, change phones, or a subscription doesn't seem to be
recognised, tap **Restore Purchases**, in settings or on the paywall itself.
The app asks the store what you own and applies it. "No purchases to
restore" means the store answered and had nothing for that account, so
check you're signed into the same Apple ID or Google account you bought
with.

The app also checks with the store every time it comes to the foreground,
so a subscription you cancel is noticed promptly, and one you renew is too.
Offline, it trusts what it last knew until that expires.

## Refunds

We don't take the money; Apple and Google do. So refunds go through them,
and we can't issue one ourselves even when we'd like to.

- **iOS:** reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find the purchase, request a
  refund.
- **Android:** Google Play, order history, find the purchase, request a
  refund. Within 48 hours it's usually automatic.

If you're not happy with the app, email
[support@cycladat.com](mailto:support@cycladat.com) as well. We'd rather know
why, and we'll help with the store side if it's being difficult.
