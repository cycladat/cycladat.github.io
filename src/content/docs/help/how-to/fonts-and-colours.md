Every sticker starts out in Abel, white. That's deliberate: it's readable on
most footage and it doesn't fight the video. It's also not what everyone
wants, so tap a sticker once and the style panel slides up from the bottom.

Styling is free. It's the export of premium stickers that costs, not how they
look.

## Fonts

The top row is ten fonts. Scroll it sideways and tap one; the sticker changes
on the spot, so you're choosing on the real footage rather than guessing from
a name.

The set is a mix on purpose. Abel, Montserrat and Bebas Neue are clean and
get out of the way. Anton is loud. Share Tech Mono, Orbitron and Audiowide
look like a head-up display, which suits power and speed. Racing Sans One and
Faster One are what they sound like. Caveat is handwriting, and it's better
on a photo than a fast descent.

## Colours

The second row is ten colours. Same deal: tap and see.

At the end of the row is the **eyedropper**, labelled "pick from video". Tap
it and the video pauses on the current frame and becomes a colour picker.
Drag your finger over it and the sticker takes the colour under your
fingertip live, with a small loupe showing you the exact sample. Lift your
finger to keep it. Matching the sticker to your jersey, the bike, or the sky
in that particular shot is the difference between an overlay and something
that looks like it belongs in the frame.

If the eyedropper isn't there, the app couldn't read frames from that video
on your device. That's rare and it only takes the dropper away; the fixed
colours still work.

## Apply to all

Once a sticker looks right, **apply to all** pushes its font and colour onto
every sticker already on the video, and onto any you add for the rest of the
session. It's the fast way to a consistent look: style one, apply, then add
the rest.

It resets when you leave the editor or start a new video, so the next clip
begins at Abel and white again.

## Closing the panel

Swipe the panel down, or tap the video above it. While the panel is open,
the swipe-up that opens the trim view is ignored, so close the panel first if
you're trying to get to the elevation strip.
