Stickers are the live readouts: a big number over a small unit label, tracking
the video as it plays. Everything about placing them is done with your fingers
on the preview, and there are only four gestures to know.

## Adding one

Tap the bike-wheel button to open the sticker drawer. The **stickers** tab is a
grid of every sticker, each previewing with sample data so you can see what
you're getting. Tap one and it lands in the centre of the video with your
ride's real data already bound to it. The drawer stays open, so you can add a
few in a row. Swipe it down, or tap the video above it, to close.

Metric and imperial are separate stickers (there's a `kph` speed and an `mph`
speed, `m` altitude and `ft` altitude, and so on). Pick the one you want to
see; mixing them on the same video is fine.

Stickers in the drawer wearing a rainbow jersey are premium. You can place
them and style them for free. Exporting a video that still has one on it is
where the purchase comes in, and the app will tell you at that point rather
than before.

## Moving

**Drag it.** Put a finger on the sticker and move it. It follows your finger
anywhere on the video. There's no snapping and no grid, so if you want it
tucked in a corner or dead centre over the road, put it there.

## Resizing

**Pinch it.** Two fingers on the sticker, spread to make it bigger, pinch to
make it smaller. The range is wide (a fifth of the default size up to six
times it), so a sticker can be a quiet caption or the whole point of the
frame. The number and the unit label scale together.

Resizing and moving can happen in the same gesture. Two fingers that spread
_and_ travel will do both.

## Removing

**Double-tap it.** The sticker disappears. There's no confirmation, because
adding it back is one tap in the drawer. If you double-tapped the wrong one,
that's the fix.

## Changing how it looks

**Tap it once** to open the style panel for that sticker: fonts, colours, and
an eyedropper for pulling a colour out of the footage. That has its own page:
[fonts and colours](/help/how-to/fonts-and-colours/).

## Where the gestures don't reach

Two things aren't sticker gestures and it's worth knowing that up front.

Swiping **up** on the video opens the trim view and the elevation strip, which
is how you cut the clip down and line the data up with the footage. See
[trimming the clip](/help/how-to/trimming/) and
[my data doesn't line up with the video](/help/troubleshooting/data-doesnt-line-up/).

The **create** button stays greyed out until at least one sticker is on the
video. An overlay with nothing on it isn't a video we can make.

If you forget any of this in the editor, the small **i** button shows the
same tips as a dialog.
