A template is a handful of stickers placed at once, arranged so they land
sensibly on any phone. It's a starting point, not a lock. The moment they're
on the video they're ordinary stickers: drag, pinch, restyle, or double-tap
away the ones you don't want.

Open the drawer and switch to the **templates** tab. Each card shows the
layout on sample footage. Tap one and the stickers appear.

## What's in them

| Template | Stickers |
| --- | --- |
| early riser | time, temperature |
| just cruisin' | speed, cadence |
| onwards and upwards | power, gradient, heart rate |
| train's movin' | power, speed, cadence |
| the big push | power, speed, cadence, heart rate |

Templates place the metric versions. If you want `mph` or `ft`, double-tap
the metric sticker off and add the imperial one from the stickers tab.

## Premium templates

A template card wearing a rainbow jersey places at least one premium sticker
(power, heart rate or gradient, as things stand). That's a heads-up, not a
gate. You can place the template, and if you remove the premium stickers
before you export, the export is free. Leave them on and the app will offer
the purchase when you tap create.

## Styling

If you've already set a look with **apply to all**, a template arrives in that
look. Otherwise it's the default, Abel and white, and you restyle the stickers
one at a time or apply again from any of them. See
[fonts and colours](/help/how-to/fonts-and-colours/).

Placing a template closes the drawer, since you've usually got what you came
for. Tap the bike-wheel button to open it again if you want to add more.
